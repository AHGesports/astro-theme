### **SEO-Optimized Multi-Language URL Structure (Research-Based):**
*Reference: [`autoflow-url-structure.md`](autoflow-url-structure.md)*

**Multi-Language Structure (EN/ES/RU) - Fortune 500 Pattern:**
- **English**: `/en/` - Primary market with full content hierarchy
- **Spanish**: `/es/` - International expansion with English slugs for stability
- **Russian**: `/ru/` - International expansion with English slugs (avoids Cyrillic encoding issues)

**Key URL Categories (Consistent English Slugs Across All Languages):**
- **Product Pages**: `/[lang]/features/`, `/[lang]/pricing/`

- **Persona Landing Pages**: `/[lang]/use-cases/` (botters, web-scrapers, growth-hackers, power-users)
- **Competitive Content**: `/[lang]/vs/` (competitor comparisons)
- **Legal & Compliance**: `/[lang]/legal/` (compliance, terms, privacy)
